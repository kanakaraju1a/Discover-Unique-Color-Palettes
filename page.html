Color Themes

Six Palettes. Six Worlds.

Each color theme captures a distinct mood and visual identity. From the cool tranquility of the ocean to the raw intensity of volcanic fire — find the palette that speaks to your creative vision.

Ocean Breeze

Serene and refreshing. Cool teals, sky blues, and soft seafoam whites evoke open water and clear horizons.

Desert Sunset

Warm and dramatic. Rich terracottas, burnt oranges, and deep purples conjure the magic of a desert dusk.

Forest Mystic

Deep and grounded. Mossy greens, earthy browns, and foggy grays create an atmosphere of ancient woodland mystery.

Urban Neon

Electric and bold. Hot pinks, electric blues, and acid greens pulse with the energy of a city after dark.

Lavender Dreams

Soft and ethereal. Dusty mauves, lilac purples, and creamy whites invite calm, romance, and quiet elegance.

Volcanic Fire

Intense and primal. Molten reds, charcoal blacks, and scorched oranges channel raw elemental power.

Typography

Font Pairings That Work

Typography is the silent voice of design. The right font pairing establishes tone, readability, and personality — before a single word is read.

Playfair Display + Lato

Elegant & Editorial. A classic serif headline paired with a clean humanist sans-serif for body text. Ideal for luxury brands, editorial layouts, and premium experiences.

Montserrat + Open Sans

Modern & Structured. Two geometric sans-serifs working in harmony — strong, confident headings with a neutral, highly legible body. Perfect for tech and business.

Cormorant + Raleway

Refined & Creative. An expressive high-contrast serif meets an elegant thin sans-serif. Evokes artisanal craft, fashion, and sophisticated creative studios.
